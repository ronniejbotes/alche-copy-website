## House style matched to the live blog

Modelled on `local-seo-for-service-businesses`:

- ~1,900-2,300 words
- Bold one-sentence answer in the opening paragraph (this is what gets pulled into AI Overviews and featured snippets)
- H2 structure with a comparison table, numbered causes/pillars, and a checklist
- Direct second-person address, authoritative but plain
- FAQ section before the close (drives FAQ schema + AI citation)
- "What We Would Tell You" section giving a straight recommendation, including when *not* to buy
- Closing CTA to `/contact`
- Related Articles block

## SEO mechanics built in

- **Question headlines** match spoken/conversational query phrasing, which is how people search in ChatGPT and Perplexity.
- **Answer-first openings** — every post leads with a direct, quotable answer sentence. This is the single highest-leverage thing for AI citation.
- **FAQ sections** on all five, ready for `FAQPage` schema.
- **Internal link mesh** — all five interlink, and all link out to existing posts and service pages. Inbound counts: `how-long-does-seo-take` 6, `how-much-does-seo-cost` 4, others 2 each.
- **Named sources** for statistics (Ahrefs practitioner poll, GoodFirms agency survey, Google's own guidance) rather than unattributed numbers. E-E-A-T signal, and AI systems preferentially cite content that attributes.
- **Honest negative framing** — each post states when the service is the wrong choice. Counterintuitively strong for conversion and for citation, because it reads as genuine advice rather than a pitch.

## Before publishing

1. **Add `FAQPage` schema** to each post's FAQ section.
2. **Set canonical URLs** and confirm slugs match the front matter.
3. **Add author bio + published/modified dates** — E-E-A-T signals that matter for AI citation.
4. **Add the reciprocal internal links** from the existing posts back to these five. Highest value: `what-is-ai-seo` → `how-long-does-seo-take`; `how-much-do-google-ads-cost` → `seo-vs-google-ads`; `what-is-a-landing-page` → `how-much-does-a-small-business-website-cost`.
5. **Re-check the statistics** before publishing — figures were gathered August 2026 and CTR/zero-click numbers move fast.

## Data caveat

The "SEO ~8x ROI vs Google Ads ~4x, $31 vs $181 CPL" figure in `seo-vs-google-ads` is single-source and averages across very different industries. It is presented in the post *with* that caveat stated explicitly rather than as fact. Keep the caveat if you edit.
