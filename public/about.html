About Us

About Position Xero
Founder-Led Performance Marketing

We don't just run ads. We build revenue machines.

Position Xero was built on one frustration: watching good businesses get burned by agencies that optimised for their own margins instead of client results.

Our Story

Why Position Xero Exists

We started Position Xero after seeing the same story play out over and over: a business owner invests $1,500 a month in Google Ads, gets a pretty report full of impressions and clicks, and still has no leads to show for it. They switch agencies. Same result. They give up on digital marketing entirely.

The problem isn't the platform. Google Ads and Meta Ads work. SEO works. Lead generation works. The problem is agencies that don't treat your money like their own, that hide behind vanity metrics and never get to the actual goal: qualified leads that turn into revenue.

Position Xero was built to be different. We measure one thing: cost per qualified lead. Everything else, impressions, clicks, reach, engagement, is secondary. We report on what actually matters to your business, and we don't stop optimising until the numbers make sense.

We're one of the few agencies offering LLM SEO as a core service, optimising your brand to be recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini alongside traditional Google ranking. Because the future of search is already here, and most of your competitors haven't noticed yet.

Where we're based, and who we work with. Position Xero is run from South Africa, and we work with service businesses internationally: the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia and elsewhere, alongside clients here at home. Everything we do (campaign builds, reporting calls, ad approvals, SEO work) is delivered remotely, which is exactly how a performance marketing engagement runs anyway: the work happens inside your ad accounts, your analytics, and your website, not in a boardroom. International clients get USD pricing and working hours that overlap their own. South African clients get local, ZAR-priced lead generation from a team in the same time zone that understands POPIA, load-shedding-driven demand, and how SA buyers actually behave. We say this plainly because an agency that's vague about where it sits is usually hiding something.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Results Over Vanity

We measure CPL, CPA, and ROAS, not impressions and reach. If it doesn't contribute to your bottom line, we don't report on it as a win.

Radical Transparency

You see everything. Every dollar spent. Every campaign result. Every optimisation decision. No black-box dashboards, no vague reports.

Always Ahead

We optimise for AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) alongside classic SEO. We're always studying what's next so you're already there when the market catches up.

True Partnership

We don't take on clients we can't help. We'd rather refer you to the right specialist than take a retainer and deliver mediocre results.

Speed of Execution

We move fast. Campaigns are live within 5 business days. Audits are delivered within 48 hours. Because in business, time is the most expensive resource.

Niche Expertise

We don't serve everyone. We focus on four industries so nothing gets lost between strategy and execution. We already know what works, and what wastes budget, in each of them.
