Lead Generation for Small Business:
7 Strategies Ranked by Speed and Cost

Lead generation for a small business comes down to running one channel that produces leads this month and one that compounds over the next year. The seven below are ranked by how fast each starts producing and what each costs to run.

Lead generation for small business comes down to two decisions: which channel produces leads this month, and which one compounds so you're not paying for every lead forever. The seven strategies below are ranked by how quickly each starts producing and what each costs to run, so you can pick one of each rather than attempting all seven at once.

What Is Lead Generation? (Definition)

Lead generation is the process of attracting and converting strangers into prospects who have expressed interest in your product or service. A lead is a person who has given you their contact details, and a qualified lead is one who has confirmed they have the budget, authority, need, and timeline to buy from you.

For most small businesses, lead generation is the single biggest lever for growth. A business with a reliable, predictable lead flow can plan, hire, and grow with confidence. Without it, revenue is erratic and growth is guesswork. Here are the seven strategies we recommend for small businesses, ranked by speed of results.

Google Ads: the fastest way to get leads for a small business

Speed: Leads within 24-72 hours. Cost: Medium to high ($500+ ad spend/month). Scalability: Very high.

Google Search ads target people who are actively searching for your exact service right now. There's no higher-intent traffic source in digital marketing. If someone searches "real estate agent Denver" or "plumber Phoenix", they're almost certainly ready to buy.

To make Google Ads work: create a dedicated landing page for each service (not your homepage), write ad copy that matches what the searcher is looking for, and track conversions so you know your cost per lead from day one.

Google Ads management starts from $850/month at Position Xero, learn more about our Google Ads service.

Google Business Profile: free local leads, if you serve an area

Speed: Results in 2-6 weeks. Cost: Free (time investment only). Scalability: Medium.

If you're a local service business, your Google Business Profile is one of the most powerful free lead generation tools available. When someone searches "attorney Dallas" or "cleaning company near me", Google Maps results appear before the organic links.

To optimise your Google Business Profile: fill in every field, add high-quality photos, list all your services, respond to every review (positive and negative), and post updates regularly. A fully completed profile gets 7x more clicks than an incomplete one (Google, 2024).

Landing pages: convert the traffic you are already paying for

Speed: Amplifies results of all other channels. Cost: One-time design investment. Scalability: High.

If your current website isn't generating leads, every dollar you spend on ads or SEO is being wasted. A landing page built for conversion, with a clear offer, social proof (testimonials, stats), and a single obvious CTA, can double or triple the conversion rate from the same traffic.

A good landing page answers three questions in 5 seconds: What do you do? Why should I trust you? What should I do next? If your current site takes longer than that to answer these, it needs work. Position Xero builds landing pages from $750.

Cold email: direct outreach when you have no audience yet

Speed: Replies within 3-7 days. Cost: Low (email tools + time). Scalability: Medium.

Cold email is one of the highest ROI lead generation channels when done correctly. The key is personalisation and a compelling offer, not a generic "Hi, we'd love to work with you" template that everyone ignores.

A high-performing cold email: references something specific about the recipient's business, identifies a specific problem they're likely experiencing, offers a specific solution with a specific outcome, and has one clear call to action (a 15-minute call, not a "let's discuss").

A well-executed cold email campaign targeting 100 ideal prospects typically generates 15-20 replies and 3-8 qualified conversations.

Meta Ads retargeting: bring back the visitors who left

Speed: Works as soon as you have website traffic. Cost: Low (small budget goes far). Scalability: Medium.

Retargeting ads on Facebook and Instagram follow up with people who visited your website but didn't convert. These audiences convert at 3-5x the rate of cold traffic, because they already know who you are.

Install the Meta Pixel on your website today. Then create a simple retargeting campaign that shows your best testimonial or offer to website visitors for the next 30 days. Even on $100-$200/month ad spend, retargeting delivers an excellent return.

Local SEO: the best long-term lead generation for a small business

Speed: 4-12 weeks depending on competition. Cost: Low to medium (ongoing). Scalability: Very high.

Local SEO targets phrases like "pool company Austin" or "recruitment agency Houston", searches made by people in your area looking for your service. Unlike ads, organic SEO rankings don't stop when you stop paying. They compound over time.

A business that ranks on Google page 1 for 5 local keywords can generate 20-50 organic leads per month at zero ongoing cost per click. It takes 3-6 months to build, but the long-term ROI of SEO beats paid advertising for most service businesses. See our full SEO services.

Referral programs: systematise the leads you already earn

Speed: 2-4 weeks to implement. Cost: Near zero. Scalability: Medium.

Referrals are the highest-converting lead type: a referred prospect already trusts you before the first conversation. But most businesses rely on occasional, unsolicited referrals instead of building a system.

A simple referral program: identify your top 10-20 clients, reach out personally to thank them for their business, and make a specific ask, "Do you know 2 or 3 business owners who are struggling with [the specific problem you solve]? I'd love an introduction." A structured ask generates 3x more referrals than a vague suggestion.

Which Strategy Should You Start With?

Need leads this week? Start with Google Ads. Have a budget but need it to work long-term? Combine Google Ads with SEO. No budget? Prioritise Google Business Profile (free) and cold outreach. Already getting traffic but no leads? Fix your landing page before anything else.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest way to generate leads for a small business?

The fastest way is Google Ads targeting high-intent keywords for your service and location. Campaigns can start generating leads within 24 to 72 hours of launch, making it the go-to strategy when you need leads immediately.

How do I generate leads without spending money on ads?

Without an ad budget, the most effective free strategies are cold email outreach, optimising your Google Business Profile, asking existing customers for referrals systematically, and publishing helpful content optimised for SEO. These take more time but require minimal financial investment.

What is the best lead generation strategy for a service business?

For service businesses, Google Ads is the fastest and most reliable strategy because it captures people actively searching for your service. Combined with a high-converting landing page, it delivers the lowest cost per qualified lead of any channel for most service businesses.

How many leads should a small business aim for per month?

Calculate your target by working backwards: if you close 1 in 5 leads and want 10 new clients per month, you need 50 qualified leads. Set your lead generation target based on your conversion rate and revenue goal, not an arbitrary number.
