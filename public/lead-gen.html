What is lead generation? Lead generation is the process of attracting and converting strangers into prospects who've expressed interest in your product or service. It involves identifying your target audience, reaching them through the right channels, and capturing their details so your sales team can follow up and close the deal.

Our Channels

How We Generate Your Leads

We use a multi-channel approach because relying on one source is a business risk. We diversify your lead flow across five proven channels. If you run a smaller operation, start with our guide to lead generation for small business.

Paid Ads (Google & Meta)

High-intent lead generation through Google Search ads and targeted Meta campaigns. The fastest way to generate a predictable, scalable flow of new leads.

Cold Email Outreach

Personalised cold email sequences targeting your ideal client profile. When done right, cold email generates some of the highest-quality leads at the lowest cost.

Cold DM (LinkedIn & Instagram)

Direct outreach through LinkedIn and Instagram to connect with decision-makers in your target niche before they enter a buying cycle.

SEO & Content

Long-term organic lead generation through content that ranks for the exact searches your ideal clients make. Compounds in value over time, unlike ads.

Landing Pages

High-converting landing pages engineered to turn traffic into leads. Split-tested headlines, compelling offers, and frictionless forms that maximise conversion rate.

Lead Qualification

We don't just hand you a list of names. Every lead is pre-qualified against your criteria before it reaches you, saving you time and improving close rates.

Our Process

The Lead Qualification Process

A lead is only valuable if it meets your criteria. Here's how we ensure quality over quantity, and how it stacks up against cost per lead benchmarks across industries.

01

Define Your Ideal Client

We start by mapping out your ideal customer profile: industry, company size, location, budget, and buying trigger. This becomes our targeting blueprint.

02

Build Qualification Criteria

We design landing page forms and pre-qualification questions that filter out unsuitable prospects before they reach your inbox, using BANT methodology.

03

Deliver Qualified Leads

Qualified leads are delivered to your CRM or email in real time. You focus on closing; we handle the top of funnel.

Once we have account access, campaigns go live within 10 business days, with a first optimisation pass 48 hours after launch and your first full cost-per-lead report at Day 30. See your first 90 days for the complete timeline, backed by our 90-day cost-per-lead guarantee.

Lead Generation Questions

Lead generation is the process of attracting and converting strangers into prospects who've expressed interest in your product or service. It involves identifying your target audience, reaching them through the right channels (ads, SEO, cold outreach), and capturing their details through landing pages, forms, or direct contact so your sales team can follow up and close.

We qualify leads through targeting criteria (ensuring only the right audience sees your campaigns), pre-qualification questions on landing pages, and initial follow-up sequences. A qualified lead meets your criteria for Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline: the BANT framework. Only prospects who pass these criteria are passed to your sales team.

Starter campaigns are typically built to target 20 to 50 qualified leads per month, depending on your industry, budget, and market. We agree on the target with you upfront and report against it openly, with no long-term contract required.

A lead is anyone who has submitted their details. A qualified lead is someone who has confirmed they have the budget, decision-making authority, genuine need, and timeline to buy your service. We build qualification into the front of our funnels so you spend your time selling to people who can actually become clients.
