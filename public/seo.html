Definitions

Understanding Modern SEO

The search landscape has changed. Here's what every business owner needs to understand.

What Is AI SEO? AI SEO is the practice of optimising your website and content so that AI systems, including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini, cite your brand as an authoritative source when users ask questions in your niche. For a full breakdown, read our deep-dive guide on what AI SEO is and how it works.

What Is LLM SEO? LLM SEO (Large Language Model SEO) is the process of structuring your content, brand mentions, and online presence so that large language models trained on web data recognise and recommend your business when generating answers about your industry or service category.

What Is Traditional SEO? Traditional SEO is the practice of optimising your website through technical improvements, keyword targeting, and link building to rank higher in Google's standard organic search results. It remains the foundation of any digital marketing strategy.

How-To Guide

How to Optimise
for AI SEO in 5 Steps

This is the exact framework we use to get service businesses ranked and appearing in Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT answers.

01

Build E-E-A-T Signals

Add author bios, credentials, and real-world evidence of expertise. AI systems favour content from demonstrably authoritative sources.

02

Implement Schema Markup

Add FAQPage, HowTo, Organization, and Service schema to your key pages. Structured data is the language AI systems read most fluently.

03

Write Direct-Answer Content

Every question should be answered directly in 40-60 word paragraphs. AI systems extract and cite concise, factual responses.

04

Build Brand Entity Authority

Get your brand consistently mentioned across authoritative external websites, industry directories, and relevant publications.

05

Optimise for Snippet Extraction

Format lists, tables, and definitions in clean HTML that's easily parsed and reproduced by AI systems generating answers.

FAQ

Common SEO Questions

Traditional SEO optimises your website to rank on Google's standard search results pages through keywords, backlinks, and technical improvements. AI SEO optimises your content so that AI-powered search systems, including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, cite your brand as an authoritative source when generating answers.

To get cited by ChatGPT and other large language models, build strong entity authority through consistent brand mentions on authoritative websites, structured content with clear definitions, and FAQ-format pages that answer questions directly. This is the core of LLM SEO, a service Position Xero specialises in for service businesses worldwide.

LLM SEO is the process of optimising your brand to be recommended by AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. It matters because AI assistants now field a large share of the questions people used to type into Google, and many buyers ask them for product and service recommendations directly. If you're not optimised for AI, you're invisible to a growing share of your market.

For most competitive keywords, ranking on Google page 1 takes 3 to 6 months with consistent SEO work. Local SEO for less competitive markets can show results in 4 to 8 weeks. AI SEO citations in Google AI Overviews typically appear within 4 to 8 weeks of structured content optimisation.

A good local SEO strategy includes: optimising your Google Business Profile, building local citations across directories, targeting location-specific keywords, earning customer reviews, and creating locally relevant content. In 2026, this should also include AI SEO to appear in Google AI Overviews for local search queries. For more answers, visit our marketing FAQ.

Google AI Overview is an AI-generated summary that appears at the top of Google results for certain queries. It synthesises answers from multiple web sources using large language models. To appear in AI Overviews, your content needs strong E-E-A-T signals, clear structured content, schema markup, and direct answers to commonly asked questions.

E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness, Google's framework for evaluating content quality. High E-E-A-T signals (author credentials, factual accuracy, external citations, brand authority) are critical for ranking in traditional search and being cited in AI-generated answers.
